▸ Revenue miss is the primary driver — spend was $74K favorable. People savings ($340K under) are absorbing cost overruns.

▸ Cash: $3.4M at April 30 (-$347K MoM). Lower collections; AR $1.2M. No credit issues flagged.

▸ Hosting: Vervint + Acronis full bill = $480K in April (+$29K over). YTD +$151K — needs budget revision.

▸ R&D Contractors: Softserve $42K open item with Ray — resolve before May close.

▸ Channel / Acronis ramp: +$27K over budget in April — confirm if recurring cost; update budget.

▸ Marketing tradeshows: +$58K over April budget — validate ROI before next tradeshow cycle.
RETENTION HIGHLIGHTS
▸ April churn spike (Billing): Unusual event — nearly 15× January level. Investigate which clients; classify as one-time or trend.

▸ Downsell at 5-month high in April — Billing largest share, PSA spiked after quiet Feb/Mar. Revenue-per-customer problem deepening.

▸ PSA upsell at 5-month low — expansion opportunities narrowing as base shrinks. PSA entering compression phase.

▸ Priority: before May close — identify April Billing churns + downsell drivers. Prioritize PSA retention plan.
