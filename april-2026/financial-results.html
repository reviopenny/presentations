APRIL RETENTION HIGHLIGHTS ▸ April churn spike (Billing): Unusual event — nearly 15× Jan level. Investigate; classify as one-time or trend.
▸ Downsell at 5-month high — Billing largest share, PSA spiked. Revenue-per-customer problem deepening.
▸ PSA upsell at 5-month low — expansion narrowing as base shrinks. Entering compression phase.
ARR Trends — TTM (4-Year View)
▸ Billing: Only ARR-positive product. GRR improving to 91%+ (best-in-class). Net retention >100%. But new ARR nearly halved YoY — a sales pipeline problem, not retention.
▸ PSA ARR declining faster each year — driver is downsell, not churn. Churn has improved 3 years running. Downsell nearly tripled. Customers staying but shrinking — pricing pressure or product-fit erosion, harder to reverse than churn.
▸ GRR divergence: Billing GRR improved from high-80s to 91%+. PSA GRR sliding below 80%. At current PSA retention, product contracts regardless of new sales volume.
▸ NRR compression is entirely PSA. Billing >100% NRR all 4 years. PSA collapsed to mid-80s — losing revenue value each year before accounting for churn. Requires high new sales to hold flat; PSA is not generating that.
